Assessments & certifications

Learn. Progress. Achieve.

Elite Coder

A structured coding assessment and certification built around CSTA K–12 Computer Science Standards. Students receive a proficiency scorecard and an e-certification from Singapore Science Centre Global at each level.

Professional Certificate

Explore programming education through UTMSPACE-recognised courses, the lifelong learning arm of Universiti Teknologi Malaysia, delivered by an appointed UTMSPACE Smart Partner.
